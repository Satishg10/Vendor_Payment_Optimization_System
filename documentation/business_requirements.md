# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Project Information

**Project Name:** Vendor Payment Process Optimization System

**Project Type:** Business Process Improvement and Data Analytics

**Department:** Finance and Accounts Payable

**Project Objective:** Improve vendor invoice processing efficiency and reduce payment processing delays.

## 2. Business Scenario

A medium-sized organization works with approximately 150 vendors and processes around 500 invoices per month.

The current vendor payment process has an average processing time of 18 calendar days.

Management has established a target of reducing the average processing time to 6 calendar days.

All business figures in this project are simulated.

## 3. Problem Statement

The existing vendor payment process experiences potential inefficiencies due to:

- Manual invoice registration
- Delayed invoice verification
- Inefficient approval workflows
- Lack of centralized invoice tracking
- Poor visibility into pending payments
- Delayed identification of processing bottlenecks

These issues can increase processing time and affect vendor relationships.

## 4. Project Objectives

1. Centralize vendor invoice information.
2. Improve invoice verification and validation.
3. Track approval responsibilities and timelines.
4. Monitor payment processing stages.
5. Identify pending and overdue invoices.
6. Measure invoice processing turnaround time.
7. Reduce average processing time from 18 days to 6 days.
8. Improve visibility through an Excel dashboard.

## 5. Project Scope

### Included

- Vendor master management
- Invoice registration
- Duplicate invoice identification
- Purchase order verification
- Goods receipt verification
- Invoice approval tracking
- Payment scheduling and tracking
- Processing time analysis
- Management dashboard

### Excluded

- Actual bank payment execution
- Integration with live banking systems
- Live ERP integration
- Real vendor financial information
- Automated GST filing

## 6. Key Business Rules

1. Every invoice must have a unique invoice reference.
2. Duplicate invoices must be flagged.
3. Invoice details must be verified before approval.
4. Purchase orders and goods receipt records must be checked where applicable.
5. Only authorized personnel can approve invoices.
6. Rejected or incomplete invoices must be sent for correction.
7. Payments must respect contractual payment terms.
8. Payment records must include actual payment dates.
9. Processing delays must be identifiable by workflow stage.

## 7. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI                     | Measurement                                         |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Total Invoices          | Count of registered invoices                        |
| Pending Invoices        | Invoices awaiting action                            |
| Average Processing Time | Average internal processing days                    |
| Overdue Invoices        | Invoices past their contractual due date and unpaid |
| On-Time Payment Rate    | Percentage paid on or before due date               |
| Duplicate Invoices      | Count of flagged duplicates                         |
| Target Achievement      | Average processing time against 6-day target        |

## 8. Success Criteria

- A centralized invoice tracking system is developed.
- Invoice verification and approval stages are traceable.
- Payment delays can be identified.
- The dashboard reports key operational KPIs.
- The redesigned workflow demonstrates a credible path towards a 6-day processing target.

## 9. Assumptions and Limitations

- All data is simulated for educational and portfolio purposes.
- The baseline processing time is assumed to be 18 calendar days.
- The target processing time is 6 calendar days.
- Processing time and contractual payment terms are measured separately.
- The project does not execute actual financial transactions.
