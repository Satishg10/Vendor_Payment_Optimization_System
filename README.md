# Vendor Payment Process Optimization System

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing and improving a simulated vendor payment process using Python, Microsoft Excel and Power BI.

The project demonstrates how data analytics can help monitor vendor invoices, track payment statuses, identify overdue payments and improve visibility into vendor payment operations.

The business scenario assumes an existing vendor payment turnaround time of 18 days, with a proposed target of reducing it to 6 days.

**Note:** The turnaround figures are simulated project assumptions and targets, not verified results from a real company.

## Business Problem

The simulated vendor payment process highlights potential challenges such as:

- Manual invoice processing
- Delayed invoice verification
- Slow approval cycles
- Lack of centralized payment tracking
- Limited visibility into pending and overdue payments

These challenges can affect operational efficiency and vendor relationships.

## Project Objectives

1. Create a structured vendor master dataset.
2. Generate and manage vendor invoice transactions.
3. Monitor invoice due dates and payment statuses.
4. Identify paid, pending and overdue invoices.
5. Analyse invoice values across payment statuses.
6. Analyse vendor distribution and vendor status.
7. Develop an interactive Power BI management dashboard.
8. Validate datasets and ensure consistency across dashboard metrics.
